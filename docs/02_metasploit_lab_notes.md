# 2. Exploitation with Metasploit – Lab Notes

## Scope
Target: Metasploitable2 in a private lab.

## Procedure Record
1. Confirm the target IP from the lab.
2. Confirm that the target is the intended Metasploitable2 VM.
3. Review the scan results and identify a known vulnerable service.
4. Use Metasploit against the authorized lab target.
5. Record the module/exploit name and target service.
6. Capture evidence of successful lab access.
7. Record basic post-exploitation information required by the task.
8. Close the session and restore the lab if necessary.

## Evidence Placeholders
- `[SCREENSHOT-01] Target identification`
- `[SCREENSHOT-02] Relevant scan result`
- `[SCREENSHOT-03] Metasploit module/configuration`
- `[SCREENSHOT-04] Successful lab access`
- `[SCREENSHOT-05] Post-exploitation system information`

## Mitigation
- Patch or replace vulnerable services.
- Remove unnecessary services.
- Restrict network exposure with firewall rules.
- Use secure service configurations.
- Monitor authentication and system activity.
